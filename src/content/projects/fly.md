Fresh Lifelines for Youth (FLY) is a nonprofit that gives legal education and mentorship to young people in the justice system. Through Develop for Good, I helped a volunteer team build its mobile app with React Native, Expo and Firebase. I set up the TypeScript monorepo and built the theme system and component library the team styles with, plus a developer guide so everyone follows the same conventions. I also wrote the Firestore schemas and seed scripts, and set up CI that lints, type-checks, tests and builds every pull request and ships releases to TestFlight. The team targets WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility.
