A little about me

My name is Abraham Hernandez. I'm a Spanish-American with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from the Rochester Institute of Technology. I was raised in San Rafael, California, found my passion for programming early on, and I now work full time as a software developer at More Vang.

One of my biggest goals is to give back by helping students discover and pursue careers in tech. Growing up, I didn't have many resources or mentors to guide me into computer science, so I had to figure a lot out on my own. I want the next generation of students, especially those from underrepresented or underserved communities, to have the support I was missing.

Away from the keyboard I garden, read sci-fi, and listen to classical and 90s rock.
